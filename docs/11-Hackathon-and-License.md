# Hackathon and license

Checked 2026-09-17. The [AssemblyAI Voice Agent Hackathon page](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon) advertises an online September 1–30, 2026 event and requires participants to build on AssemblyAI. This planning repository is not yet a working submission.

The [general lablab guide](https://lablab.ai/guide) requests an online working prototype, video presentation and pitch deck. Its [general hackathon guide](https://lablab.ai/guide/ai-hackathons) additionally describes a public GitHub repository, MP4 video up to five minutes and PDF deck. Treat those as general preparation guidance; the event's submission form controls exact fields and limits.

## License decision and evidence limit

The root `LICENSE` applies MIT to original EasyLedger contributions. MIT is a chosen permissive baseline, not a claim that the accessible AssemblyAI event page explicitly mandates it. Other official lablab events, such as [Co-Creating with GPT-5](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/co-creating-with-gpt-5), publish MIT requirements, but those are precedent rather than rules for this event.

The linked [rule book](https://lablab.ai/hackathon-rules), [submission guidelines](https://lablab.ai/delivering-your-hackathon-solution) and [terms](https://lablab.ai/terms-of-use) returned no readable rule text through the research tool. Verify the current event-specific clause and submission form before publishing the final entry. External API terms and dependency licenses remain separate; do not imply ownership of AssemblyAI services, Graphify assets, third-party libraries or source pages.

## Submission checklist

- [ ] Each member registered and team entry established.
- [ ] Confirm final deadline/timezone and event-specific eligibility, reuse and licensing rules.
- [ ] Demonstrate actual AssemblyAI-powered entry, correction and dashboard tool calls.
- [ ] Provide usable hosted prototype with clearly labeled synthetic data and safe demo access.
- [ ] Prepare video and pitch deck; check exact current format/length requirements.
- [ ] Publish reviewed source repository with LICENSE and dependency notices; no credentials or merchant data.
- [ ] Record which work existed before the event and what was built during it.
- [ ] Explain limitations honestly, including English voice MVP and incomplete revenue data behavior.

Presentation structure: merchant problem → speak sales → inspect computed receipt → correct quantity → watch chart change → compare products → arrange/save dashboard → explain architecture and measured results. The briefs' four judging categories are pitch framing; verify any event-specific weighting rather than inventing a score rubric.
